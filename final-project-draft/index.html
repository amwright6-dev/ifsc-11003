Sonic is a platforming video game franchise created and produced by Sega. It eventually became a multimedia franchise. The Sonic series is one of the most popular and iconic gaming franchises. Introduced in 1991, the series stars Sonic the Hedgehog and Dr. Eggman. Most of the games involve collecting rings, destroying TV power-ups/bubbles (in most of the 2D Sonic games and Sonic Adventure 1, 2 and Sonic 06), defeating enemies and bosses. It's also a physics-based platformer. There are 5 powerup boxes in Sonic 1, Sonic 2 and Sonic CD. The Ring powerup box gives you 10 rings when destoryed. The Shield powerup box gives you a Shield that protects you from 1 hit. The Invincnibity powerup box gives you invninicbity for a limited amount of time. It protects you from hazards and enemies. The Speed Shoes powerup box gives you increased speed and jump height for a limited amount of time. This also speeds up the music. The Extra Life powerup box gives you an extra life. You also get an extra life for collecting 100 rings. For example, in Sonic 1, you had to use the level design and terrain to your advantage. You can roll on slopes to go even faster! Sonic 2 introduces the Spindash, which allows you to rev up a charge. However, you are required to come to a stop. You can make a stronger Spindash by adding more revs (before releasing). In order to Spindash, you need to press down and press the jump button. This also defeats enemies. The Peel-Out was introduced in Sonic CD, which is a even faster Spindash. However, you are completely vulnebrable during it. You can roll during a Peel-Out, being invulnerable at the cost of losing speed. Jumping and rolling are not only main mechanics in the games, but it also defeats enemies. If you bounce from an enemy and hold the jump button, you retain your previous height. If you don't hold the jump button, you will only get a small bounce. This also works on TV powerups. Sonic 3 introduces the Insta-Shield and the Elemental Shields. Only Sonic can use the abilities of the new powerups in Sonic 3. The Insta-Shield extends Sonic's hitbox and tempoprilay provides invincitbity frames, allowing him to destory powerup boxes and enemies. Certain enemies require the Insta-Shield to be defeated. Otherwise, you will lose rings. The Fire Sheild protects you from fire and gets disabled by water. It can also make an air-dash. The Aqua Sheild prevents you from drowning. It also gives you higher bouces from jumps. The Electric Shield protects you from electricity and gets disabled by water. It attracts nearby rings and allows you to double jump. Originally in Sonic 3, Tails is able to fly. Tails was introduced in Sonic 2, where he had a slighty smaller hitbox compared to Sonic. This allows him to get through certain parts of Sonic 2 faster. Sonic 3 also introdcues Knuckles. Knuckles is playable in Sonic and Knuckles. (Also in Sonic 3 and Knuckles) Knuckles has a slighty shorter jump compared to Sonic and Tails. He can also glide and climb walls. If you were just in the air, but defeated an enemy by gliding, you will bounce really high. This also works for powerup boxes. If you touch the ground while gliding, you can't do anything for about 1 second. The levels in the original Sonic games are called Zones, which are seperated by Acts. Sonic 1 and Sonic CD has 3 Acts or Rounds per Zone, while Sonic 2 and Sonic 3K has 2 Acts per Zone. There are bosses at the end of every 3rd Act (or every 2nd Act from Sonic 2 and Sonic 3K) Sonic 3K introduced bosses for every 1st Act. Sonic 3 also has unique themes for every 2nd Act. Metal Sonic and Amy Rose was introduced in Sonic CD. Sonic CD has 4 seperate layouts and themes for every non-boss Act. Only 2 layouts and themes for every boss Act. In Sonic 1, every 3rd Act would contain an actual level before the boss fight. Compare that to Sonic CD, which only has some actual level before the boss fight. The Sonic 1 case also applies to Sonic 2 and Sonic 3K. Usually in the original Sonic games, every Zone will have unique mechanics and gimmicks. There are checkpoints in most of the levels. After you activate a checkpoint, you will respawn at the most recent checkpoint when you die. Rings are also the health system.
