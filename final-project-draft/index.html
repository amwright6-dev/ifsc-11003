Sonic the Hedgehog

Sonic is a platforming video game franchise created and produced by Sega. It eventually became a multimedia franchise. The Sonic series is one of the most popular and iconic gaming franchises. Introduced in 1991, the series stars Sonic the Hedgehog and Dr. Eggman. Most of the games involve collecting rings, destroying TV power-ups/bubbles (in most of the 2D Sonic games, Sonic Adventure 1 and 2, and Sonic 06), and defeating enemies and bosses. It's also a physics-based platformer.

There are 5 power-up boxes in Sonic 1, Sonic 2, and Sonic CD. The Ring power-up box gives you 10 rings when destroyed. The Shield power-up box gives you a Shield that protects you from 1 hit. The Invincibility power-up box gives you invincibility for a limited amount of time, protecting you from hazards and enemies. The Speed Shoes power-up box gives you increased speed and jump height for a limited amount of time, which also speeds up the music. The Extra Life power-up box gives you an extra life. You also get an extra life for collecting 100 rings.

In Sonic 1, you had to use the level design and terrain to your advantage, such as rolling on slopes to go even faster. Sonic 2 introduces the Spindash, which allows you to rev up a charge. However, you are required to come to a stop. You can make a stronger Spindash by adding more revs before releasing. In order to Spindash, you need to press down and press the jump button. This also defeats enemies. The Peel-Out was introduced in Sonic CD, which is an even faster Spindash. However, you are completely vulnerable during it. You can roll during a Peel-Out, making you invulnerable at the cost of losing speed.

Jumping and rolling are not only main mechanics in the games, but they also defeat enemies. If you bounce from an enemy and hold the jump button, you retain your previous height. If you don't hold the jump button, you will only get a small bounce. This also works on TV power-ups.

Sonic 3 introduces the Insta-Shield and the Elemental Shields. Only Sonic can use the abilities of the new power-ups in Sonic 3. The Insta-Shield extends Sonic's hitbox and temporarily provides invincibility frames, allowing him to destroy power-up boxes and enemies. Certain enemies require the Insta-Shield to be defeated; otherwise, you will lose rings. The Fire Shield protects you from fire, gets disabled by water, and allows you to perform an air-dash. The Aqua Shield prevents you from drowning and gives you higher bounces from jumps. The Electric Shield protects you from electricity, gets disabled by water, attracts nearby rings, and allows you to double jump.

Tails was introduced in Sonic 2, where he had a slightly smaller hitbox compared to Sonic, allowing him to get through certain parts of the game faster. Originally in Sonic 3, Tails also gained the ability to fly. Sonic 3 introduces Knuckles, who is playable in Sonic & Knuckles (as well as in Sonic 3 & Knuckles). Knuckles has a slightly shorter jump compared to Sonic and Tails, but he can glide and climb walls. If you are in the air and defeat an enemy by gliding, you will bounce really high. This also works for power-up boxes. However, if you touch the ground while gliding, you can't do anything for about 1 second.

The levels in the original Sonic games are called Zones, which are separated by Acts. Sonic 1 and Sonic CD have 3 Acts (or Rounds) per Zone, while Sonic 2 and Sonic 3K have 2 Acts per Zone. There are bosses at the end of every 3rd Act (or every 2nd Act in Sonic 2 and Sonic 3K). Sonic 3K introduced bosses for every 1st Act, and Sonic 3 also has unique themes for every 2nd Act.

Metal Sonic and Amy Rose were introduced in Sonic CD. Sonic CD has 4 separate layouts and themes for every non-boss Act, and only 2 layouts and themes for every boss Act. In Sonic 1, every 3rd Act contains an actual level before the boss fight. Compare that to Sonic CD, which only has a small level section before the boss fight. The Sonic 1 structure also applies to Sonic 2 and Sonic 3K.

Usually, in the original Sonic games, every Zone has unique mechanics and gimmicks. There are checkpoints in most of the levels. After you activate a checkpoint, you will respawn at the most recent checkpoint when you die. Rings also act as the health system.
